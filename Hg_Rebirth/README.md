
**Hg Unit Frames** brings the familiar look of **Perl Classic Unit Frames** to modern World of Warcraft, with a fresh implementation built from scratch around the Mainline API.

Designed for **Retail and Forever**. Classic clients will never be not supported.

---

**Hg Unit Frames** is still in **Beta**!

Please report any bug or request any feature [here](https://github.com/HgAlexx/hg-unit-frames/issues) :)

---

**Features**

- Familiar Perl-style unit frames, with compact layouts and customizable health and power text.
- Adjustable frame positions, scales, portraits, colors and visibility.
- Positioning mode with sample frames, plus sticky links to keep related frames together.
- Configurable buffs and debuffs, with filters, timers and cooldown displays.
- Per-character settings, character copying, built-in and custom presets.
- Optional automatic preset switching for solo play, groups and different instance types.
- Modular design: enable only the displays you want.

---

**Supported unit frames & display**

- **Player**: Your health, power and class resources, with experience or watched reputation.
- **Player Aura**: Configurable player buffs, debuffs and weapon enchants.
- **Player Pet**: Your pet's health and power, with an optional pet-target frame.
- **Target**: Your selected target's vitals and identity, with classic and alternative layouts.
- **Target Aura**: Configurable buffs and debuffs around the target frame.
- **Target of Target**: Compact displays for your target's target and the next target in the chain.
- **Focus**: A dedicated frame for keeping track of your focus.
- **Focus Aura**: Configurable buffs and debuffs around the focus frame.
- **Focus Target**: A compact display for your focus's current target.
- **Party**: Party-member frames with horizontal or vertical layouts and optional embedded pet health bars.
- **Party Target**: Separate target frames for each party member.
- **Party Pet**: Dedicated frames for party members' pets.
- **Cast Bars**: Casting and channeling displays integrated into Player, Target, Focus and Party frames.

---

**Configuration**

All options are available under **Settings > AddOns > Hg Unit Frames**.

Open configuration with `/hguf`, the minimap button, or the AddOn compartment entry. Each display has its own settings panel, while common settings and presets make it easy to configure the whole interface together.

Use **Positioning mode** to arrange frames and preview their layouts without needing a target, pet or group.
